About

The person behind the camera.

Hi — I'm Madeson. I photograph real life across the Sacramento Valley: families in the orchards at golden hour, kids under Friday night lights, companies celebrating a big year, bands mid-encore, graduates finally holding the thing they worked so hard for.

I grew up here, and it shows in the work. I know which fields glow at 7pm in October, which venues have tricky light, and how to get a genuine smile out of a seven-year-old who has decided smiling is over.

What my clients seem to value most isn't just the photos — it's that the whole thing was easy. I answer messages quickly, show up early, keep sessions fun, and deliver when I say I will. Your moment matters to you, so it matters to me.

— Madeson Bea
