The experience

Hiring a photographer shouldn't feel complicated.

Here's exactly what happens from your first message to the day your gallery arrives — so there are no surprises, just good photos.

01

Tell me about your shoot

Fill out the short inquiry form — your date, what you're planning, and anything you're wondering about. I reply personally within 24 hours.

02

We plan the details

We'll pick a location and time (I'll suggest spots with the best light), talk outfits, and I'll answer every question. A small retainer saves your date.

03

Your session

Relaxed and unhurried. I guide you with easy prompts, keep kids entertained, and quietly catch the real moments in between.

04

Receive your images

A sneak peek within a few days, then your fully edited gallery in a private online gallery within 2–3 weeks — download, share, and order prints from anywhere.

Everything people ask

Honest answers to the usual questions.

We're not "photo people." Will this be awkward?

That's most of my clients, honestly. The first five minutes are for warming up — after that, everyone forgets the camera. If you can hang out with your people, you can do this.

What should we wear?

Once you book, you'll get a short style guide with what photographs well (and what to avoid). If you're stuck, send me photos of options and I'll help you choose.

What about bad weather or a rained-out game?

We reschedule at no charge. Valley weather is usually on our side, but when it isn't, we find a better day.

How do payments work?

A small retainer holds your date, with the balance due around session day. Invoices are online and take a minute to pay.

How do we get our photos?

Through your own private, password-protected online gallery. Download the full-resolution files, share the link with family, and order professional prints directly.

Sound easy? It is.
